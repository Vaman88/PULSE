Privacy overview.

Your account and files

The backend stores hashed credentials, session information, and your generated plan, including up to 30 calendar days of daily stock and sales summaries per medicine for the charts. Sales CSVs and fitted models are processed in server memory and discarded. Inventory entries and current quantities are also saved under your account. Signing out ends your session; your saved plan and chart summaries remain attached to your account.

Use & limitations.

Research use

PULSE ratings and pharmacy estimates are experimental, not validated clinical or purchasing guidance. Review source dates, validation errors, stock constraints, deliveries, and supplier timing before making decisions.

How scores work

Each disease has a model rating from 1 to 10 for each horizon. These are ratings, not probabilities.

Documented review levels

3
Sporadic international occurrence.
5
Substantial international transmission or sparse U.S. reports.
6
A U.S. outbreak beginning amid widespread international activity.
7
Arkansas presence or ongoing disease risk.
8
Multiple Arkansas cases or reports.
9
A spreading, uncontained Arkansas outbreak.

Levels 1, 2, 4, and 10 are not defined in the available review notes. These descriptions summarize the review log; current model predictions and saved labels do not consistently follow the intended rubric.

Sources: dataset output review provenance; Arkansas scoring diagnostic.
